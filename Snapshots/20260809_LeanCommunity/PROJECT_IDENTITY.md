# CNNA (ComplemeNt Net Architecture) — From Primitive Provenance to Mathematical Structure

**Paper title:** *From Primitive Provenance to Mathematical Structure: A Response-Coupled Derivation on a Growing $b$-ary Tree*

**CNNA** expands to **ComplemeNt Net Architecture**. The internal capital `N` is deliberate: the project studies one growing provenance **net** through a recurring complement architecture. At every relevant stage, the same historically grown structure is resolved into a realized or retained part, a stage-relative complement, and an interface or handoff through which effective response and further growth are determined.

The architecture begins at the origin of the derivation. `C001` fixes the empty baseline; `C002` performs the first realization by creating the root; `C003` and `C018` expose the complement of not-yet-realized continuation slots; `C004A` selects the first such slot; and `C013`/`C014` turn it into the first nontrivial response-capable directed net. Later cuts, Schur/DtN elimination, record/live divergence, nested refinement, and OQS/AQFT specializations are successive mathematical realizations of the same complement principle.

`Complement` is not an intrinsic label on a node and is not restricted to a set-theoretic binary complement. It is cut-, scale-, stage-, or projection-relative. A coordinate retained at one resolution may belong to the eliminated complement at another.

CNNA is the current finite, deterministic, response-coupled specialization of the broader research framework **Generalized Open Provenance Systems**. The universal completion claim is a research hypothesis, not a theorem of the current DAG. Finite POVM, open-quantum-system, Weyl/GNS, and modular/AQFT constructions are progressively later specializations and retain their individual proof status.

The stable software namespaces remain `CNNA` and `CNNAProofs`.

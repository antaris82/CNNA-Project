# ComplemeNt Net Architecture (CNNA)

CNNA is the throughgoing architecture in which one historically grown provenance net is repeatedly resolved into a realized or retained part, a cut- or stage-relative complement, and an interface through which effective response and the next birth are determined.

## Origin

The architecture begins with the transition from the empty carrier to the first realized node, becomes structurally explicit when the b-ary grammar distinguishes the born prefix from open continuation slots, and becomes a nontrivial response-capable net with the first non-root birth and its directed relation.

## Relative-complement guard

Complement is relational and cut-, scale-, or projection-relative; it is not an intrinsic permanent partner assigned to each node.

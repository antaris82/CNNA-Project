\section*{Conceptual scope: generalized open provenance systems}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conceptual scope: generalized open provenance systems}

The project is organized around the following research hypothesis.  An empirically open system may admit a \emph{provenance-complete} extension in which its dynamically relevant history is represented as part of the real state, the observed subsystem is selected by a cut, and its effective response is obtained by eliminating or compressing the complementary degrees of freedom.  In schematic form,
\[
  \mathfrak P_n=(\Gamma_n,\mathsf{Rec}_n,\mathsf{Live}_n,\mathcal K_n,\mathcal C_n,\mathcal O_n),
\]
where $\Gamma_n$ is an event-provenance DAG, $\mathsf{Rec}_n$ is immutable birth data, $\mathsf{Live}_n$ is the current response-bearing state, $\mathcal C_n$ is a cut, and $\mathcal O_n$ is an observation or coarse-graining channel.  This is a \emph{programmatic hypothesis}, not a theorem that every empirical open system possesses such a completion.

\begin{cnnaInfoBox}{Formal status of the generalization}
The current DAG proves only its registered finite CNNA specialization.  The weak generalized hypothesis permits intrinsically stochastic provenance-complete dynamics.  The stronger deterministic hypothesis claims that all apparent stochasticity is produced by partial observation, unresolved provenance, a cut-relative complement, coarse graining, or uncontrolled intervention.  The stronger statement is not established by the present DAG.
\end{cnnaInfoBox}

For a retained region $B_n\subseteq V_n$, the complement $I_{B_n}=V_n\setminus B_n$ is interpreted as the environment relative to that cut.  In the present finite directed-linear specialization, complement elimination is the exact unique-solve Schur/DtN operation
\[
  \Lambda_{B_n}=K_{BB}-K_{BI}X,\qquad K_{II}X=K_{IB}.
\]
This response reduction must not be identified with a quantum partial trace, a conditional expectation, or a stochastic marginalization.  Those are mathematically different reductions that may instantiate the same abstract open-provenance role only after their own hypotheses are supplied.

The response-coupled CNNA update has the derived order
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \text{provenance state}&\longrightarrow\text{canonical cut}
  \longrightarrow\text{effective response}\\
  &\longrightarrow\text{steering}
  \longrightarrow\text{birth instruction}
  \longrightarrow\text{record/live update}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Later sections test the coherence of nested cuts, projective carriers, refinement maps, and cocycles.  A finite response-derived POVM exists in the retained Legacy construction, but its migration into the derived-only DAG remains an explicit future proof chain: \[
\begin{aligned}
  \text{positive compressed response state}
  &\longrightarrow \text{frame effects}
  \longrightarrow \text{POVM completeness}\\
  &\longrightarrow \text{Born evaluation}
  \longrightarrow \text{projective coarse graining}.
\end{aligned}
\]  No current node is relabelled as having proved a quantum instrument or an open-system limit.

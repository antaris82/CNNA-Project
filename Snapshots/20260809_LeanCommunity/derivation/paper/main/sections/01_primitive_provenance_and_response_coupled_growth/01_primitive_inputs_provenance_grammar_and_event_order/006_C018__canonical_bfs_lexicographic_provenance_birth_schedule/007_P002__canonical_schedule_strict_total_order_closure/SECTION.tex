\paragraph*{Position and proof responsibility.}
P002 is the static proof-certification child of C018.  It exports one stable
contract for the breadth-first/lexicographic provenance order.  The contract
contains no born-state, unsaturation, least-open, or executable-selector
hypothesis.  Those state-dependent statements are owned by C004 after C005 has
introduced the response-capable state.

\paragraph*{Formal contract.}
For provenance addresses $a,c$, C018 defines the strict order
\[
  a\prec c
  \iff
  |a|<|c|\;\lor\;\bigl(|a|=|c|\land a<_{\mathrm{lex}}c\bigr).
\]
P002 packages irreflexivity, transitivity, asymmetry, trichotomy, and
comparability of distinct addresses.  This is the standard finite-word
lexicographic setting, composed here with the separate depth comparison to form
the C018 breadth-first/shortlex schedule.
% CNNA-EXTREF-BEGIN EXT-USE-P002-WORDS-MAIN
\cite[Ch.~1, finite words and lexicographic order]{Lothaire1997CombinatoricsWords}.
% CNNA-EXTREF-END EXT-USE-P002-WORDS-MAIN
For C018 open-slot records it packages
irreflexivity, transitivity, and asymmetry of the induced order.  Because a
slot record contains proof-bearing cutoff data, totality is stated
extensionally:
\[
  s\prec_{\mathrm{slot}}t
  \;\lor\;
  \operatorname{child}(s)=\operatorname{child}(t)
  \;\lor\;
  t\prec_{\mathrm{slot}}s.
\]
Thus P002 claims a strict total order on selected child addresses, not
proof-irrelevant equality of arbitrary slot records.

\paragraph*{Minimal selected-child uniqueness.}
For an explicitly supplied predicate $Q$ on C018 slot records, define
\[
  \operatorname{Minimal}_Q(s)
  \iff
  Q(s)\land \forall t\,[Q(t)\Rightarrow\neg(t\prec_{\mathrm{slot}}s)].
\]
P002 proves that two $Q$-minimal witnesses select the same child address.  The
proof uses the C018 comparison theorem for distinct selected children: either
one witness precedes the other, contradicting the corresponding minimality
clause, or their selected children are equal.

\paragraph*{Necessity and scope checks.}
The extensional equality alternative is necessary: two proof-bearing records
may select one child without being definitionally equal.  Total comparison is
necessary for minimal-witness uniqueness.  Conversely, a static total order
does not define the least address not yet born; that theorem requires the C005
born prefix and remains at C004.  P002 therefore creates no backward dependency
on recurrent-state data.

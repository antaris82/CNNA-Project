\cnnaProofNodeHeading{025}{P001}{Reusable directed Schur/DtN/Kron channel closure}
\cnnaNodePlacement{1.3.9.1}{D2}
P001 is the unique proof owner behind the C006--C007--M003--M004 channel.
The mathlib-free Core supplies exact finite directed states, cuts, responses,
steering values, and the birth-law relation.  The separate pinned
\texttt{CNNAProofs} package proves the analytic and finite-combinatorial
closure without introducing a Core-to-proof import.

\paragraph*{Formal contract.}
For an ordered boundary/interior block family, P001 assumes only
nonpositive off-diagonal entries, exact row conservation, positive
interior-to-boundary reachability, and a positive path from the distinguished
boundary port to a different boundary port.  It proves exact Core-to-rational
semantic agreement, existence and uniqueness of the interior solve, response
existence and witness independence, the directed-Laplacian sign and
conservation laws, nonnegative response diagonal, and strict positivity of the
distinguished diagonal.  No symmetry, inverse, pseudoinverse, regularization,
extra grounding vertex, or independent connectivity axiom is added.

\paragraph*{Proof architecture.}
The proof sequence is explicit.  S01 identifies the Core exact-fraction and
matrix operations with rational values.  S02 proves the directed maximum
principle and triviality of the zero-boundary interior kernel.  S03 uses the
finite-dimensional injectivity--surjectivity bridge to obtain the unique
interior solve.  S04 proves response existence and representative
independence.  S05 proves nonpositive off-diagonal response entries, row
conservation, and diagonal nonnegativity.  S06 derives strict distinguished
positivity by propagation along a positive path to a second boundary port.
S07--S09 derive the canonical parent coordinate, exact C007 matrix laws, and
provenance-backbone reachability directly from C005, M001, M002, and C007.
S10 exposes the P001-to-M003/M004 supporting theorems without changing either Core
constructor.  The public closure modules above P001 consume these theorems.  S11 independently instantiates the same generic closure on a
two-boundary/one-interior bidirected-chain family.

For the independent family with strictly positive rational weights $g_L,g_R$,
\[
K_{BB}=\operatorname{diag}(g_L,g_R),\qquad
K_{BI}=\begin{pmatrix}-g_L\\-g_R\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K_{IB}=\begin{pmatrix}-g_L&-g_R\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K_{II}=(g_L+g_R).
\]
Its sign, row-sum, and path hypotheses are proved directly.  The closure theorem
then invokes only \texttt{directedSchurDtnClosure}.  The reuse therefore does
not depend on \texttt{ResponseCapableState}, the next slot, provenance
addresses, M001, or a C007 realization.

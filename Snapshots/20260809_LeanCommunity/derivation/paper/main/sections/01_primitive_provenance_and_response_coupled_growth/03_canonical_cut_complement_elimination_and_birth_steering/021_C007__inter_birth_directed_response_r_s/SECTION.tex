\cnnaNodeHeading{021}{C007}{Inter-birth directed response $R_n(s_{n+1})$}
\cnnaNodePlacement{1.3.6}{D1}
Let $X_n$ be a recurrent C005 state with $n\geq 1$, let $s_{n+1}$ be its
unique C004 next open provenance slot, and let
\[
  \mathcal C_n(s_{n+1})=(B_n,I_n)
\]
be the canonical ordered M001 cut.  The C005 current carrier is partitioned
exhaustively by $B_n\sqcup I_n$, while the unborn child of $s_{n+1}$ belongs to
neither block.

For each stored directed conductance $c_n(u,v)>0$, C007 fixes the
source/out-degree matrix convention
\[
  K_n(u,u)=\sum_{v}c_n(u,v),
  \qquad
  K_n(u,v)=-c_n(u,v)\quad (u\neq v).
\]
The row coordinate is therefore the conductance source and the column
coordinate its target.  This is not a tunable sign convention: it is the direct
matrix realization of the ordered pair $(u,v)$ already stored by C005.  No
transpose, in-degree alternative, symmetrization, geometric factor, external
port, or response-independent bias is inserted.

% CNNA-CONTEXT-BEGIN CTX-C007-LAPLACIAN
The resulting matrix is a standard nonsymmetric directed Laplacian: off-diagonal entries are nonpositive and every row sums to zero.  The scientific choice that matters here is not the existence of such a Laplacian, but the fixed source/out-degree orientation inherited from the stored conductance pairs.

% CNNA-EXTREF-BEGIN EXT-USE-C007-LAPLACIAN-DIRECT
\cite[Abstract and Sec.~1]{AgaevChebotarev2005NonsymmetricLaplacian}.
% CNNA-EXTREF-END EXT-USE-C007-LAPLACIAN-DIRECT
% CNNA-CONTEXT-END CTX-C007-LAPLACIAN


Order the complete born carrier as $B_n$ followed by $I_n$ and write
\[
  K_n=
  \begin{pmatrix}
    K_{BB}^{(n)} & K_{BI}^{(n)}\\
    K_{IB}^{(n)} & K_{II}^{(n)}
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Because this order is inherited from M001, C007 introduces no new coordinate
permutation.  On the exact M002 domain, equivalently when
\[
  K_{II}^{(n)}X=K_{IB}^{(n)}
\]
has a unique exact fraction-value solution, the inter-birth directed response is
\[
  \boxed{
  R_n(s_{n+1})
  =K_{BB}^{(n)}-K_{BI}^{(n)}X
  }.
\]
This is precisely the C006 Schur/DtN response of the C005 state-dependent block
realization.

% CNNA-INFOBOX-BEGIN INFO-C007-MARKOV-GENERATOR
\begin{cnnaInfoBox}{The directed Laplacian is minus a Markov generator}
\label{box:c007-markov-generator-infobox}
Define $Q_n:=-K_n$.  Then for $u\ne v$, $Q_n(u,v)=c_n(u,v)\ge0$, while $Q_n(u,u)=-\sum_v c_n(u,v)$ and every row sums to zero.

% CNNA-EXTREF-BEGIN EXT-USE-C007-MARKOV-INFOBOX
\cite[Ch.~2, continuous-time Markov chains]{Norris1997MarkovChains}.
% CNNA-EXTREF-END EXT-USE-C007-MARKOV-INFOBOX

This is exactly the sign pattern of a continuous-time Markov-chain generator.  The same directed weights can therefore be read either as conductances in $K_n$ or transition rates in $Q_n$.

The comparison is deliberately one-way: CNNA does not thereby introduce probabilities, waiting-time distributions, or a stochastic clock.  It exposes a reusable algebraic bridge for later analysis.

\textbf{Status.} Exact Sign Duality Without Stochastic Postulate. The sign duality is exact under the C007 row/source convention; any stochastic interpretation would require additional normalized state semantics.
\end{cnnaInfoBox}
% CNNA-INFOBOX-END INFO-C007-MARKOV-GENERATOR


The temporal statement is literal: $R_n(s_{n+1})$ is measured after the first
$n$ non-root births and before birth $n+1$.  The next child is not a coordinate
of $K_n$ or $R_n$.  C007 does not yet convert this matrix into a scalar steering
functional and does not execute a growth update; those operations belong to
M003 and M004.

The construction remains partial exactly on the M002 domain.  C007 neither
replaces exact unique solvability by a condition-number threshold nor asserts a
stronger invertibility theorem without proof.  For the finite C005 fixtures
covered by the executable audit, including asymmetric conductances and extra
born--born directed edges, all 99 tested reachable pre-birth states lie in that
exact domain.

\paragraph*{Exact-value output semantics.}
The displayed matrix is an elementwise exact fraction value. A Lean raw fraction matrix is identified internally by cross multiplication. Python returns normalized \texttt{Fraction} representatives; their agreement with Lean is an explicit executable cross-language verification, not a theorem about core \texttt{Rat} arithmetic.  Response uniqueness is therefore value uniqueness, not structural equality of a particular numerator--denominator encoding.

\paragraph*{Verified closure and limits.}
The implementation proves response existence and value-level uniqueness on
exact M002 domain membership and excludes the unborn child from both block
coordinate sets.  C007 fixes only the pre-birth matrix response.  Scalar
steering, robustness transforms, directed birth bias, and the transition to
$X_{n+1}$ remain downstream.

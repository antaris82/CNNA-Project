\cnnaNodeHeading{018}{M001}{Canonical birth-local measurement cut $\mathcal C_n(s_{n+1})$}
\cnnaNodePlacement{1.3.4}{D2}
Let $X_n$ be a recurrent state admitted by C005 and let
$s_{n+1}=(p,\rho,c)$ be the unique next open provenance slot identified by
C004.  Here $p$ is the already-born provenance parent, $\rho$ its local
sibling rank, and $c=p\frown\rho$ the not-yet-born child address.

M001 defines birth locality using only the finite provenance relations
already present at this stage.  Write
\[
A(p)=\{\epsilon,\,p|_1,\ldots,p\}
\]
for the root-to-parent prefix chain, and
\[
S_{<}(s_{n+1})=\{p\frown j:0\le j<\rho\}
\]
for the earlier same-parent sibling slots.  These are the two provenance roles
directly resolved by the next slot: its causal prefix chain and the already
resolved local ranks preceding it.  No weights are used in their definition.

Let
\[
V_n^{\mathrm{ord}}=(\epsilon,v_1,\ldots,v_n)
\]
denote the C005 carrier in its inherited C018 order.  Define the ordered
boundary and interior by restriction of this already-fixed order,
\[
B_n(s_{n+1})
 =\bigl.V_n^{\mathrm{ord}}\bigr|_{A(p)\cup S_{<}(s_{n+1})},
\qquad
I_n(s_{n+1})
 =\bigl.V_n^{\mathrm{ord}}\bigr|_{V_n\setminus B_n(s_{n+1})},
\]
and set
\[
\boxed{\mathcal C_n(s_{n+1})=(B_n(s_{n+1}),I_n(s_{n+1}))}.
\]
Thus M001 does not introduce a second ordering convention: each block is an
ordered subsequence of the C005 carrier.

By the C005 initial-prefix property and the C004 least-open characterization,
$p$ and every earlier sibling $p\frown j$ with $j<\rho$ are already born.  The
prefix ancestry of $p$ is already born by provenance-parent closure.  Hence
$B_n(s_{n+1})\subseteq V_n$.  The C004 child $c$ is absent from $V_n$, so it
occurs in neither block.  Boundary and interior are disjoint and exhaust the
entire born carrier.  Consequently the displayed rule determines a unique
finite ordered cut from $(X_n,s_{n+1})$.

The word ``canonical'' is relative to this frozen provenance selector: M001 is
the minimal cut retaining exactly the causal-prefix and earlier-sibling roles
of the next slot.  It does not claim that no other provenance-defined cut can
be written down.  In particular, M001 introduces no sampling cap, geometric
radius, terminal frontier, response-dependent selection, or downstream
spacetime notion.

The cut is still purely combinatorial.  C006 acts only after such an
ordered boundary/interior pair has been supplied, and M002 owns the
separate question whether the corresponding interior block lies in the exact
Schur/DtN domain.

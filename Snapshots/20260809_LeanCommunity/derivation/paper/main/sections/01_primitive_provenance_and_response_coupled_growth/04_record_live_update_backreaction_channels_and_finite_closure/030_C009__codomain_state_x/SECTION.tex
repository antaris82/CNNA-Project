\paragraph*{Position and role.}
C009 is the deterministic assembly boundary between one valid C005 state and
its raw one-step codomain.  It consumes the already selected C004 slot, the
M004 birth instruction, and the separately closed C016/C017 record/live
projections.  It introduces no new response law and does not re-prove any
upstream invariant.

\paragraph*{Assembly contract.}
For a response-capable state $X_n$, a proof-bearing canonical next slot
$s_{n+1}$, coherent current channels, and an M004 instruction $B_n$, C009
constructs
\[
  D_{n+1}=\bigl(\mathcal S_n,\,B_n^{\mathrm{born}}\mathbin{\|}[s_{n+1}],\,
    R_{n+1},\,L_{n+1}\bigr),
\]
with
\[
  R_{n+1}=\mathrm{C016}(R_n,B_n),\qquad
  L_{n+1}=\mathrm{C017}(L_n,B_n).
\]
The schedule is inherited literally, the born non-root list is extended by
exactly the selected child, and the record/live components are exactly the
already-verified C016/C017 projections.  The C005--C017 handoff additionally
requires that the pre-step live channel represents the ordered current C005
conductance list.

\begin{cnnaInfoBox}{Assembly is not recurrent closure}
C009 proves existence and uniqueness of the raw codomain data for one fixed
admissible input.  It deliberately does not prove that this raw object again
satisfies every C005 \texttt{ResponseCapableState} invariant.  That statement is
T002.  If T002 needs a missing fact whose semantics belongs to C004, C005,
M004, C016 or C017, that fact must be closed at its point of origin rather than
hidden in T002 or retrofitted into C009.
\end{cnnaInfoBox}

\paragraph*{Canonicity and representative independence.}
For every fixed proof-bearing input, the raw codomain assembly exists and is
unique.  Replacing exact-fraction representatives by C006-\texttt{SameValue}
equivalent representatives changes neither schedule nor born prefix and yields
\texttt{SameValue}-equivalent record/live outputs.
